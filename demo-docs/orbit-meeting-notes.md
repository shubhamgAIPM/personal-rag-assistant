# Orbit Notes: Planning Meeting Notes (Q3)

*Fictional sample document for the public demo. Orbit Notes is not a real product.*

## Attendees

Priya (product lead), Marcus (engineering lead), Elena (design), Tom (support).

## Decisions

1. **Beta date moved.** The private beta moves from October to November. The main reason is that decision extraction needs another month of testing before it is reliable enough for customers.
2. **Pricing confirmed.** The team plan stays at 8 dollars per member per month. A proposal to charge 12 dollars was rejected because competitors are cheaper.
3. **Calendar integration postponed.** The calendar integration is postponed until after the public launch, to keep the first release small.
4. **Free plan limit.** The free plan will allow up to 5 members and 100 notes, as in the brief.

## Action items

- Marcus will improve decision extraction accuracy and report results by the end of September.
- Elena will finish the note editor designs by 15 September.
- Priya will recruit 20 teams for the private beta.
- Tom will write the beta onboarding guide.

## Risks discussed

- Extraction accuracy might not reach the target in time. If it does not, the beta will launch with manual decision entry only.
- Recruiting 20 teams may take longer than planned.

## Next meeting

The team meets again in two weeks to review extraction accuracy.

## Budget review

Marcus reported that the engineering budget for the quarter is 90,000 dollars, of which 61,000 dollars has been spent. The largest remaining cost is the language model usage for decision extraction. Priya asked for a cap of 1,500 dollars per month for beta usage. The team agreed on the cap and on a weekly report of actual usage.

## Customer feedback summary

Tom summarised feedback from the 12 teams that tried the early prototype:

- Ten teams liked the plain-language search and described it as "the part that feels like magic".
- Seven teams wanted a calendar integration, which is why the decision to postpone it was discussed at length.
- Five teams found the extracted decisions too detailed, and asked for shorter summaries.
- Three teams asked for a way to export all notes, which is already planned.

## Hiring

The team will open one new engineering role in October, focused on search quality. The role will report to Marcus. Elena asked to add a part-time contractor for illustration work during the launch period, and Priya agreed to review the budget before approving it.

## Open questions

- Should the free plan include search, or only note taking? No decision was made.
- Should decision summaries be shortened by default? Elena will test two versions with the beta teams.
