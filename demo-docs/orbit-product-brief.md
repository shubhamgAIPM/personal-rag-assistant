# Orbit Notes: Product Brief

*Fictional sample document for the public demo. Orbit Notes is not a real product.*

## Problem

Small teams keep meeting notes, decisions and action items in many places: chat threads, personal notebooks and shared drives. When someone asks "what did we decide about pricing?", nobody can find the answer quickly, and people end up repeating discussions.

## Target users

Orbit Notes is for project leads and product managers on teams of 5 to 30 people. They run several meetings a week and need a reliable record of decisions that the whole team can search.

## Product vision

Orbit Notes captures meeting notes in a simple editor, automatically extracts decisions and action items, and lets anyone search them in plain language. The goal is that a new team member can find the reason behind any past decision in under one minute.

## MVP scope

The first release includes:

- A Markdown note editor with meeting templates.
- Automatic extraction of decisions and action items.
- Plain-language search across all notes in a workspace.
- Sharing a workspace with up to 30 members.

## Out of scope for the MVP

- Calendar integrations.
- A mobile app.
- Audio recording and transcription.
- Custom roles and permissions.

## Pricing

The team plan costs 8 dollars per member per month. A free plan covers one workspace with up to 5 members and 100 notes.

## Timeline

The private beta is planned for October. Public launch is planned for January.

## Customer research findings

We interviewed 24 project leads between March and May. Nineteen of them said they lose track of decisions at least once a month. Fourteen said the biggest cost is repeating a discussion that already happened. Only six use any dedicated tool for notes; most rely on shared documents and chat history.

When asked what would make them switch, the most common answer was "search that actually finds the thing I half remember". The second most common was "no extra work after the meeting".

## Competitive landscape

The main alternatives are general-purpose document tools and chat-based note bots. General-purpose tools are flexible but have weak search for decisions. Chat-based bots capture text but do not organise it. Orbit Notes focuses on one job: making past decisions easy to find and trust.

Competitor team plans range from 5 to 10 dollars per member per month, which is why the price is set at 8 dollars.

## Success metrics

The team will judge the MVP on four measures:

- At least 60 percent of beta teams still active after 8 weeks.
- Median time to find a past decision under 60 seconds.
- Decision extraction accepted without edits at least 85 percent of the time.
- At least 30 percent of workspaces invite 5 or more members in the first month.

## Onboarding flow

A new workspace starts with a sample meeting note so that search and extraction can be tried in the first minute. The product then asks the lead to paste or write their next real meeting notes. An email on day 3 shows the first extracted decisions, and an email on day 7 shows how to search across notes.

## Risks

- People may not trust automatic extraction, which is why a person confirms each decision.
- Teams with strong existing habits may not switch. The free plan lowers the cost of trying.
- Search quality depends on note quality, and very short notes give little to search.
